Leonida Map Companion – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Leonida Map Companion ("the app") is an unofficial, fan-made map app published by DestecGames. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any game publisher.

Data the app stores on your device

Your map settings (visible categories and which locations you marked as found) are stored only on your device. We do not have accounts and we do not receive this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Location data downloads

The app may download an updated list of map locations from our server. This is a normal web request; no personal information is sent with it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, in order to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud.

  • On iOS you choose whether to allow tracking via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not use your advertising identifier for tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, we ask for your consent using Google's consent management (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy Settings" in the app's menu.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
